# What is ICM

Indian cryosphere meet or ICM is an annual conference of leading glaciologist from India and abroad, discuss the new advances and collaboration in the field of glaciology. It is organised by the Indian branch of International Glaciological Society. This was the third time it was being held after being held in IIT Ropar and Bombay. This meet is mostly focused on glaciologist mostly from remote sensing and modelling background. People also present and talk about their field work on glaciology. 
It is the third time it was happening and IIT Rorkee was the organising body especially the civil department. Prof. Saurabh Vijay was such an humble organiser taking care of everyone's need. 

# How did I got into glaciology

Well I had my experience of myself learning about glaciers, ice sheets, permafrosts etc. but I never studies them formally. Apparently glaciology is not being taught as a discipline in India, even though it is such an important aspect of our nation. 
So many of our great rivers are fed by these glaciers, on which millions of people depend. 
So, I was going through my Geospatial Technology course taught by Prof. Remya SN and I had to do a final project for that course. I had ideas about choosing a topic but my encounter with news piece related to GLOF event caught my attention. I thought why not apply my freshly aquired remote sensing and spatial skills here.
