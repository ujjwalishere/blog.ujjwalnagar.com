I recently to present my work at 3rd Indian Cryosphere Meet organised by International Glaciological Society at IIT Rorkee. This was my first ever conference and I had a wonderful experience. It was my first ever introduction to an academic community other than my campus. I met so many amazing people from all over India and had many great discussion. I learned so much in just two days which I would have learned in years. It was interesting to listen to pioneers of Glaciology in India and the their wisdom. Prof. Saurabh Vijay was such an humble organiseer.

## What is ICM

Indian Cryosphere Meet (ICM) is an annual conference of leading glaciologist from India and abroad, discuss the new advances and collaboration in the field of glaciology. It is organised by the Indian branch of International Glaciological Society. This was the third iteration after being held held at IIT Ropar and  IIT Bombay earlier. The main agenda of this meet was to bring together early career researchers mostly from remote sensing and modelling background. 


## How did I got into glaciology

Well I had my experience of myself learning about glaciers, ice sheets, permafrosts etc. but I never studies them formaly. Apparently glaciology is not being taught as a discipline in India, even though it is such an important aspect of our nation.
So many of our great rivers are fed by these glaciers, on which millions of people depend.
So, I was going through my Geospatial Technology course taught by Prof. Remya SN and I had to do a final project for that course. I had ideas about choosing a topic but my encounter with news piece related to GLOF event caught my attention. I thought why not apply my freshly aquired remote sensing and spatial skills here.
