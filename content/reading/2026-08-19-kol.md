Found this book in library by chance and loved it. I think I have heard or seen this book somhewhere but I am not able to recall. I loved it so much that I just read it in one sitting.  
This book is filled with beautiful illustrations and minimalistics information. It was more of like a Children's Book which makes you just keep reading it. I actually love reading children's books, they are lovely. This book is a great example of how the climate communication should be, simple so that even a layman could understand. 

Lovely and beautiful, enjoyed it.
