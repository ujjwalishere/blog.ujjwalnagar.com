Well, now it seems long back but I know it is not as old as I might feel. People who know me might not know about it, or maybe I might have brushed over the conversation as trivial. But this is one of moment in time I feel, which shaped me forever. I spent almost a year in the infamous city of Kota which is located in the state of Rajasthan. The city was said to be where the greatest minds needs to pass through, atleast that's what I was told. 

Well if you grow up in a village, you do not have a lot of role models to start with. The ones you have, maybe they should not be role models for any good society. This all I am talking about from a male and a comparatively privileged persepective. As you move up the ladder of education, you begin to wonder what to do next, as we all do. That is when you need role models and I had none. I tried to find, in my neighbours, my relatives but something did not felt right. I mean making money should not be ultimate goal of your life, right? So, all my models were inside the books. But all of them seemed people from tales, even though it was said they all were like us, walked on the same earth as we do. Looking back I feel, no child should be deprived of good role models. 

So, I did my best and followed this one person who seemed to know what he was doing. He is an intelligent and humble guy, but I won't name him here. He was different from all my friends, he used to talk about science and things that I thought wasn't able to talk to anyone. He said going to IIT is the best way, if you want a "good paltform" to start with. I never believed in this, but at that time this seemed to be the most sensible thing to do. Honestly, when I first heard about IIT, I didn't even knew that it is a university just like another government university we have nearby. It was told as something that you do. Like "Wo to IIT Kar rha" or "Uska to IIT nikal gya", forget about the full-form and all. Then it did not take me long, given my all self aquired internet skills on what it actually is. 

Going this way made more sense, some of my role models were from IITs and ofcourse all these millionaires, startups and money is there. This seemed the most approachable and doable thing, rather getting stuck with government exams and fooling your relatives, saying you are doing "Competition ki Taiyaari". One thing that nobody told me that there is a difference between science and engineering out there. For me, these were inspereable. I love making, repairing, breaking things and knowing how they work. How things work? Science!, 

Having this in mind that I am going to do cool sciency thing, I decided that I am also going to go to IIT. A followed the same person and went to kota, because as it was said "Hoga to Kota se hi hoga". Later i also realized there is another version of this which is, "Kota sa bhi nhi ko to kabhi nhi hoga iska", which nobody told me earlier. The second one was used to taunt people into and making them feel worthless. 

I was very enthusiasting when I was going, especially with the attention you get from parents and relatives, it felt great. With my mom sweets in my hands, I went to conquer IIT. I wish to say one thing, kota is a beautiful city, and more beautiful are the local people. I honestly did not had any bad experience, and it is I would say a relatively safe city. Summers can be little bit tough, but since it not as humid, summers are surviavble. The economics of whole city runs on coaching but is also famous for kota marble which is famous for flooring. It also rains good amount of time throughtout the year, but somehow you see a lot of water even in not so much rain. I think it is my theory only, but the rocky land of the city, does not hold on to much of water. 

I was enrolled in one the biggest and oldest coaching mafia, which was "the green tshirts". Just kidding, I know smart people will figure it out what it means and if you ever been to kota, you know it. They have this massive building complexes 6-7 story tall, filled with only students. I used to live near samrat chowk, Mahaveer Nagar and my coaching was not walkable from there. That area is hub for this coachings with these so many huge building, overflowing with students. Roads gets blocked when students get out from these buildings. You will only students flowing like molecules of water. 

In each one of these buildings you had atleast 4 massive hall type classrooms on each floor, where each succesive row was higher and higher. Last low was so high, the instructor never taught with their necks straight. All the people who had given up on life, used to sit on last benches. When classes start everyone was so determined that all of them are going to be AIR 1's. I wondered from the first day, that this is not statistically possible at all. I hope they never got the sense to apply the probability module on themselves, or maybe all of us were so egotistical, that we all felt that we will be able to crush each other. That was the environment all along and that was what you were told, your friend is the enemy. 



The first experience when you step out on a train station, probably before than that, you'll encounter it. The relentless bombardment of your field of view with their advertisemnt. Does not matter where you look, it was everywhere. Those big hoarding with young men with AIR 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9..... seemed like gods. They were really, they seemed big, eternal and inreachable. What you some small creature can expect to be atleast be part of that race, and just get somewhere. Where, we don't know.
Who know, maybe other people definitely would right? Maybe I am the most lost of all of them. The more I talked, the more i realised that these ones are more confused than me because atleast I know I am confused. It will be injustice to say that I have more clarity now than earlier but atleast.
