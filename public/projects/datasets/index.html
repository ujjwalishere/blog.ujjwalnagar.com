Useful datasets

Posted on Aug 11, 2025

Socioeconomic Data

Dataset Format Notes
National Family Health Survey (NFHS) CSV Data on health and family in India.
PLFS XLSX, PDF Labour force datasets.
MOSPI Survey XLSX, PDF Contains all the surveys, including PLFS.
SEDAC CSV Socioeconomic and climate data.
Census of India Various Demographic and socioeconomic data for India.
Socioeconomic High-resolution Rural-Urban Geographic Platform for India (SHRUG) Various comprising dozens of datasets covering India’s over 500,000 villages and 8000 towns over a span of 25 years.
CEDA Ashoka University

GIS & Administrative Boundaries

Dataset Format Notes
Survey of India — Official Indian Boundaries .gpkg, .shp Official boundaries from Survey of India (district, taluk, village level).
GADM .gpkg, .shp, .geojson, .kmz Boundaries for countries and subdivisions.
GeoBoundaries .geojson, .shp Political and administrative boundaries globally with simplified files.
GAUL CSV UN FAO administrative boundaries from multiple agencies.
Natural Earth CSV Cartography-ready data endorsed by NACIS.

Climate & Weather Data

Dataset Format Notes
Copernicus CSV ESA satellite imagery and environmental monitoring.
EO Browser CSV Sentinel Hub Earth observation data browser.
CIESIN Climate Data CSV Columbia University climate data portal.

Environmental Data

Dataset Format Notes
Bhuvan CSV ISRO’s geospatial platform with satellite imagery.
EM-DAT csv datasets on disasters

Remote Sensing

Dataset Format Notes
OpenTopography Various All kinds of topography data; very easy to download.
USGS Earth Explorer Various Best place to download Landsat and SRTM data.
Copernicus Browser Various All Sentinel data, Copernicus DEM, and other datasets from the Copernicus programme.
MODIS Various Moderate-resolution Earth observation products.
Radar Data Sets - ASF Various NISAR, ALOS Palsar, Sentinel-1, and older SAR datasets.
Bhoonidhi CSV All Indian remote sensing datasets.
NEO CSV NASA Earth observations visualization.

Energy, Electricity & Emissions

Dataset Format Notes
International Energy Agency (IEA) CSV Data on energy for all countries.
EIA (Energy Information Administration) Various Data from the US government, including other countries.
National Power Portal Various Real-time, daily, monthly, and annual data.
Merit India Various Real-time power generation and cost of power.
Grid India Various National power distribution company with additional data series related to the National Power Portal.
Robbie Andrew CSV Norwegian researcher collecting and sharing energy, electricity, and climate data for India.
Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas PDF, CSV Indian ministry data often appears in less user-friendly PDF formats.
World Bank Open Data CSV World development and energy indicators.
International Monetary Fund (IMF) CSV Macro-financial and energy-related indicators.
RBI Various Reserve Bank of India datasets and statistical tables.

Derived Data

Dataset Format Notes
Our World in Data Various All kinds of data.
CHIRPS CSV Global rainfall data.
GHSL CSV Population density, building density, and socioeconomic spatial raster data.
HydroSHEDS CSV Hydrological data.
AppEEARS CSV NASA Earth data extraction tool.
NASA EarthData CSV NASA Earth observation data hub.
